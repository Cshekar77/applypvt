How It Works

Three simple steps to apply

From registration to counselling, the entire process is online. Here's what you need to do.

01

Register

Create your account using your email address. Verify it instantly with a one-time OTP sent to your inbox.

02

Fill Application

Enter your personal details, Class 10 & 12 marks, and category. Submit your application before the deadline.

03

Verification

The admin team reviews your submitted details. You'll be notified once your application is verified.

04

Merit Rank

After verification, merit ranks are assigned based on your Class 12 percentage. View your rank on the dashboard.

05

Counselling

Attend the live counselling session. Seats are allotted by rank — Government and Management quota separately.

06

Admission

Once allotted, complete the fee payment and document submission to confirm your admission.

Detailed Procedure

Complete admission process

Everything you need to know before applying — step by step.

1

Create Your Account

Visit the portal and click "Create Account". Enter your full name, email, and set a password. Verify your email via OTP.

2

Login & Start Application

Log in with your credentials and click "Apply Now" from your dashboard to open the application form.

3

Fill Personal Details

Enter your full name, date of birth, gender, contact number, address, and category (General/SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PWD).

4

Enter Academic Marks

Provide your Class 10 and Class 12 board, school name, passing year, and percentage marks accurately.

5

Submit Application

Review all details carefully before submitting. You can edit your application until the form closes.

6

Wait for Verification

The admissions team will verify your application. Check your dashboard for status updates regularly.

7

Check Merit Rank

Once verified, your merit rank will appear on your dashboard based on Class 12 percentage.

8

Attend Counselling

Join the live counselling session on the scheduled date. When your rank is called, choose your quota and confirm the seat.

9

Seat Allotment

Seats are allotted from Government and Management quota separately by category. Your allotment letter will be available to download.

10

Confirm Admission

Complete the fee payment, submit original documents to the college, and confirm your admission before the deadline.

Eligibility

Who can apply?

Check the eligibility criteria before you start your application.

Academic Requirements

  • Passed Class 12 (10+2) from a recognised board
  • Minimum 45% marks in Class 12 (40% for reserved categories)
  • Mathematics or Computer Science as a subject is preferred
  • Class 10 passing certificate required
  • Appearing candidates may apply provisionally

Documents Required

  • Class 10 marksheet & certificate
  • Class 12 marksheet & certificate
  • Category/Caste certificate (if applicable)
  • EWS / PWD certificate (if applicable)
  • Transfer certificate from last institution
  • Valid government photo ID proof
